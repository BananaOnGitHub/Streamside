the requested unbuffered visible range and sampled visible count. Kind 9 rows

range or membership coverage partial; missing/invalid range is unavailable.

accessors, polling or layout requests are added. idCache and measurements remain

two list scopes share the unchanged 128-entry / 2 ms callback allowance.

position in rounded logical points. Flags distinguish no current measured-cache
entry, exact zero, invalid value and unavailable access. Missing cache entry
does not prove the row was never measured before a cache clear. Known geometry

with comparable identities, and unknown otherwise. Anonymous labels stay local
to the Arm session; raw IDs/content never leave the adapter.
